About Our Company

Portmaster Maritime Services delivers premier port operation support and marine cargo handling solutions. Based in Dipolog City, we specialize in seamless terminal management, vessel transitions, and safe dockside activities. Our experienced workforce ensures that container cargo, bulk loads, and freight move quickly and securely between vessels and land transportation hubs.

Core Capabilities

Stevedoring Services

Expert handling, securement, loading, and discharging of breakbulk, project cargo, and containers on ocean-going vessels.

Dock & Wharfage Works

Comprehensive shore-side support including mooring operations, terminal cargo sorting, marshalling, and heavy machinery support.

Logistics Coordination

End-to-end oversight ensuring swift vessel turnaround times while maintaining strict adherence to local port regulations and international safety standards.
